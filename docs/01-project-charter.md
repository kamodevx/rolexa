# Project Charter

## Project Name

Rolexa

---

## Problem Statement

Graduates and job seekers often apply for numerous job opportunities across multiple platforms, making it difficult to effectively organise and monitor their application progress. As the number of applications increases, it becomes challenging to remember where applications were submitted, track their current status, manage interview schedules, store application documents, and follow up with employers.

The lack of a centralised system often leads to missed deadlines, forgotten interviews, duplicated applications, and limited insight into the overall job search process. Existing methods, such as spreadsheets, notes, or email folders, can become difficult to maintain as the job search grows.

There is a need for a simple, organised, and reliable platform that enables job seekers to manage their career application journey efficiently while providing visibility into their progress and helping them stay organised throughout the recruitment process.

---

## Project Vision

Rolexa aims to become a modern career management platform that empowers job seekers to organise, monitor, and improve their job search journey from a single, intuitive application.

The project seeks to provide users with a structured and reliable way to manage applications, interviews, documents, reminders, and career progress while reducing the stress and complexity associated with searching for employment.

By combining organisation, visibility, and actionable insights, Rolexa strives to help users stay focused, make informed decisions, and confidently progress towards securing their next career opportunity.

---

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of Rolexa are to:

- Provide a centralised platform for managing job applications.
- Enable users to track the progress and status of each application throughout the recruitment process.
- Allow users to organise important information such as interview schedules, application documents, and personal notes.
- Improve the job search experience through search, filtering, and analytics features.
- Reduce the risk of missed interviews, follow-ups, and application deadlines.
- Demonstrate professional software engineering practices by following the complete Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).
- Build a production-ready portfolio project that showcases backend engineering, frontend development, cloud engineering, DevOps, testing, and software architecture skills.

---

---

## Functional Requirements

Rolexa shall provide the following core functionality:

### User Management

- Users shall be able to register an account.
- Users shall be able to log in securely.
- Users shall be able to log out.
- Users shall be able to update their profile information.

### Job Application Management

- Users shall be able to create a new job application.
- Users shall be able to view all their job applications.
- Users shall be able to update an existing application.
- Users shall be able to delete an application.
- Users shall be able to view detailed information about an application.

### Application Tracking

- Users shall be able to track the status of each application.
- Users shall be able to record important application dates.
- Users shall be able to store notes related to each application.

### Interview Management

- Users shall be able to schedule interviews.
- Users shall be able to update interview details.
- Users shall be able to record interview outcomes.

### Document Management

- Users shall be able to upload CVs and cover letters.
- Users shall be able to associate documents with job applications.

### Search and Filtering

- Users shall be able to search applications.
- Users shall be able to filter applications by status.
- Users shall be able to sort applications by date or company.

### Dashboard and Analytics

- Users shall be able to view application statistics.
- Users shall be able to view interview statistics.
- Users shall be able to monitor their job search progress.

### Notifications and Reminders

- Users shall be able to create reminders.
- The system shall notify users of upcoming interviews and follow-up dates.

---

## Non-Functional Requirements

Rolexa shall meet the following quality requirements:

### Performance

- The system shall provide a responsive user experience during normal operation.
- The system shall efficiently retrieve and display user data.

### Security

- The system shall authenticate users securely.
- The system shall protect sensitive user data.
- Users shall only be able to access their own information.

### Reliability

- The system shall provide consistent and reliable functionality.
- The system shall minimise data loss through appropriate error handling.

### Maintainability

- The project shall follow clean architecture principles.
- The codebase shall be modular, readable, and easy to maintain.
- The project shall include clear documentation.

### Scalability

- The system shall be designed to support future feature expansion.
- The architecture shall support increasing numbers of users and applications.

### Usability

- The user interface shall be simple and intuitive.
- Navigation shall be clear and consistent.

### Compatibility

- The application shall support modern desktop and mobile web browsers.

### Testing

- The project shall include automated testing where appropriate.
- New features should be tested before release.

### Documentation

- The project shall maintain up-to-date technical documentation throughout development.

---

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP) Scope

The first release of Rolexa will focus on delivering the core functionality required for users to effectively manage their job applications.

### Included in the MVP

- User registration and authentication.
- User profile management.
- Create, view, update, and delete job applications.
- Track application statuses.
- Record application notes.
- Search and filter applications.
- Basic dashboard displaying application statistics.

### Excluded from the MVP

The following features are intentionally excluded from the initial release and may be considered for future versions:

- AI-powered career recommendations.
- CV and cover letter analysis.
- Email and SMS notifications.
- Calendar integrations.
- Browser extensions.
- Mobile applications.
- Recruiter and company portals.
- Team collaboration features.
